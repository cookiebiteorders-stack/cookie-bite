# 🍪 Cookie Bite — "Our Story" Page Enhancement Plan

> **Brand Essence:** Fun meets Luxury | Accessible Indulgence | Warmth + Sophistication

---

## 1. Page Objective

Transform the "Our Story" page from a static brand introduction into an **immersive, emotional journey** that converts visitors into loyal fans. Every section should make the reader *feel* the Cookie Bite promise before they ever take a bite.

**Core emotional target:** "Luxury you enjoy… not something that intimidates you."

---

## 2. Page Structure & Section Blueprint

### Section 1 — Hero: The Opening Statement

**Current State:** Likely a basic headline with brand name.

**Enhancement Plan:**
- Full-viewport hero with a **luminous, bright-premium product shot** — cookies in slow-motion, light catching the chocolate drip
- Headline using **Playfair Display** in cream tones over a warm caramel/cream gradient background
- Copy: *"Born from a passion for the perfect bite."*
- Subtext: *"Cookie Bite began with a simple, yet profound idea — to transform the humble cookie into an experience of joyful luxury."*
- Soft animated entrance: text fades up, image blooms in gently
- **No dark backgrounds.** Bright, airy, luminous — the anti-moody-luxury

**Visual Direction:**
- Background: Cream `#F8F5EE` with soft caramel `#D2A47B` gradient wash
- Accent: Gold `#C8B59F` shimmer overlay on product image
- Typography: Playfair Display (headline) + Montserrat (subtext)

## 3. Design System Reference

### Color Usage on This Page

| Color | Hex | Role on Page |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#F8F5EE` | Page background, cards, hero |
| Caramel | `#D2A47B` | Primary text accents, buttons, dividers |
| Soft Gold | `#C8B59F` | Pull-quote borders, CTA outlines, icon accents |
| Blush | `#F5D9D9` | Vision card, timeline alternates |
| Mint | `#D9F5E8` | Mission card, timeline alternates |
| Soft Peach | `#F5E8D9` | Values section background |

### Typography Rules

| Element | Font | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero Headline | Playfair Display | Bold | 56–72px |
| Section Headlines | Playfair Display | Regular/Bold | 36–48px |
| Pull Quotes | Playfair Display | Italic | 24–28px |
| Body Copy | Montserrat | Regular | 16–18px |
| Captions / Labels | Montserrat | Medium | 12–14px |
| Playful Accents | Pacifico | Regular | Limited — CTAs or tags only |

### Graphic Motifs to Integrate

- **Chocolate Drips** — use as section dividers between major story beats
- **Cookie Bite Cutouts** — use as bullet icons, timeline markers, card decorations
- **Rounded Soft Blobs** — use as background shapes behind value cards and pull quotes
- **Subtle Noise Texture** — apply on full-width statement sections for depth

## 4. Motion & Interaction Guidelines

| Interaction | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Page Load | Hero image blooms in; headline fades up with 0.3s delay |
| Scroll Reveal | Sections fade + translate up (20px → 0) as they enter viewport |
| Value Cards | Gentle lift on hover (translateY -6px + shadow deepens) |
| Timeline Steps | Sequential fade-in as user scrolls through |
| CTA Buttons | Soft scale on hover (1.02x) + caramel background deepens |
| Pull Quote | Subtle shimmer animation on the gold left border |

---

## 5. Photography Direction for This Page

All images should follow the **Bright Premium** photography style from the Cookie Bite brand book:

- **Hero:** Cookies in a flatlay on cream linen, backlit with golden hour light
- **Origin Section:** Hands crafting a cookie — intimate, warm, close-up, natural light
- **Emotional Section:** Slow chocolate drip on a stacked cookie — luminous, indulgent, mouth-watering
- **Closing Section:** Open gift box revealing cookies — packaging hero, clean marble background

**Avoid:** Dark moody backdrops, heavily edited/artificial colors, stock-looking images

---

## 6. Tone of Voice — Copy Guidelines

Every sentence on this page should feel:

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Warm and sensory | Cold and corporate |
| Short and impactful | Long and dense |
| Playful and inviting | Stiff and formal |
| Emotionally focused | Ingredient/feature-focused |
| Conversational | Distant or intimidating |

**Brand Vocabulary to use throughout:**
*Joyful, Indulgent, Playful, Premium, Warm, Irresistible, Crafted, Delightful, Cherished, Luminous*

---

## 7. Mobile Experience Notes

- Hero: Single column, full-bleed image below headline
- Values: Horizontal scroll carousel (snap-scroll)
- Timeline: Vertical stacked steps with connecting line
- Vision/Mission cards: Stacked, full-width
- All CTAs: Full-width buttons on mobile, minimum 48px tap target
- Typography scales: Hero 40px / Headlines 28px / Body 16px

---

## 8. SEO & Accessibility

- **Page Title:** `Our Story | Cookie Bite — Premium Cookies, Joyful Luxury`
- **Meta Description:** *"Discover the story behind Cookie Bite — where fun meets luxury in every perfectly crafted cookie. Indulge in accessible elegance, one bite at a time."*
- All images must have descriptive **alt text** (e.g., "Stack of Cookie Bite chocolate chip cookies with dripping chocolate on a cream background")
- Color contrast must meet **WCAG AA** minimum on all body text
- Timeline and card sections must be keyboard-navigable
